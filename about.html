About VORSO

Built by three people who wanted more from fitness.

We're a small, independent team on a simple mission: make training feel like a game you actually want to win — and do it without selling anyone's data.

Our story

Why we built VORSO.

Every fitness app turns your workout into a chore — a checklist to tick, a streak to protect, another subscription to cancel. We wanted the opposite. We wanted training to feel like a game you actually want to win.

So we built one. Four ways to play: a shuffled deck of cards that sets your reps, a two-player duel of nerve where you call your number and hit it exactly, a GPS chase against a target, and a race to a new personal record. Wrapped around all of it — leaderboards, challenges, and a feed where friends can take on your best and try to beat it.

And we built it privacy-first, on purpose. Your GPS route and your form videos never leave your phone. Your real name is never shown — you pick a username. No ads, no trackers, no data sold. Competition shouldn't cost you your privacy.

VORSO is live on the App Store today, with Android and a lot more on the way. We're just getting started — and we'd love you along for it.
