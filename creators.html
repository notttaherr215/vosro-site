Why creators like you

Your workouts are already content.

We're not chasing mega-accounts. A creator whose community actually trains with them is worth more to us than a million passive followers — because your people will do the thing, not just double-tap it.

A natural fit

You already post workouts. VORSO gives that content an interactive hook: instead of watching your set, your followers open the app and play your exact challenge — then post their time back at you.

Easy to stand behind

Free, no ads, no trackers, no data sold. Your GPS route and form videos stay on your device, and your real name is never shown — you pick a username. Privacy-first is a story your audience respects.
